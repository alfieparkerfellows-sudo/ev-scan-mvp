Using EV Scan

Terms of Use

EV Scan is a free buying-assistance tool designed to make used EV information easier to understand. Accounts are optional and exist to save useful information between visits; you do not need an account to use the core scanning experience.

1. Informational tool, not a guarantee

EV Scan provides information, estimates, comparisons and buying prompts. It does not guarantee that a vehicle is safe, mechanically sound, accurately described, fairly priced or suitable for you.

A Deal Score, recommendation, battery confidence indicator, insurance estimate or similar output is an aid to decision-making, not a promise that you should buy or avoid a particular vehicle.

2. Verify important information

Before purchasing a vehicle, you should independently verify important information and consider an appropriate physical inspection, test drive and provenance/finance/theft/write-off check. Where battery condition matters, obtain suitable battery evidence or an independent battery test rather than relying on an estimate alone.

3. Data can be missing, delayed or wrong

EV Scan may use information supplied by users, sellers, public bodies and authorised third-party data providers. Data feeds can be incomplete, unavailable, delayed, incorrectly matched or changed after a report is generated.

When information is unavailable, EV Scan aims to show it as Unknown or Not available instead of inventing an answer. Verified, estimated and seller-supplied information should not be treated as equivalent.

4. Optional accounts

You may create an account to save scans, maintain a shortlist, compare saved cars, store a driving profile, use My Garage and remember appearance preferences. Account features are optional and may change as the product develops.

You are responsible for keeping your sign-in details secure and for the accuracy of information you choose to save. EV Scan may limit or suspend access where necessary to protect the service from abuse or security threats.

5. My Garage and reminders

My Garage can store dates such as MOT expiry, service dates, tax/vehicle-duty renewal dates and battery-warranty end dates. These reminders are convenience tools only. You remain responsible for checking official records, manufacturer information and legal deadlines.

An in-app or exported calendar reminder may be missing, delayed, dismissed by your device or based on a date you entered incorrectly. Do not rely on EV Scan as the sole method of meeting an MOT, tax, service, warranty or other deadline.

6. Battery and range information

Battery State of Health can only be treated as measured when supported by appropriate evidence. Modelled battery-health ranges, degradation curves and real-world range figures are estimates and can vary materially with temperature, speed, driving style, charging behaviour, tyre/wheel specification, vehicle condition and other factors.

7. Insurance estimator

Any insurance figure shown by EV Scan is an indicative estimate, not an insurance quote. Actual premiums depend on insurer underwriting, address, driver history, vehicle details, cover options, market conditions and other information. Do not rely on the estimate as confirmation that cover is available or what it will cost.

8. Marketplace listings and photos

Listings, photos, prices and seller information remain the responsibility of the relevant marketplace, advertiser or seller. EV Scan does not own or sell the vehicles it analyses. A listing can change or disappear after a scan.

9. Partner and affiliate links

Some links may be advertising or affiliate links. EV Scan may receive commission if you click or buy through certain clearly identified partner links. Commercial arrangements must not alter the Deal Score or the substantive buying verdict for a vehicle. See the Affiliate Disclosure.

10. Availability

We aim to keep the service reliable, but EV Scan may be interrupted by maintenance, software faults, third-party outages, rate limits or changes to external APIs. We may change, suspend or remove features where necessary.

11. Acceptable use

Do not misuse EV Scan, attempt to bypass security or rate limits, interfere with the service, submit unlawful content, or use the service to harvest or identify personal information about vehicle owners.

12. Liability

Nothing in these terms excludes liability that cannot legally be excluded. Subject to that, EV Scan is provided as a free informational service and you remain responsible for your purchasing decisions and for independently verifying material information before committing money.

How EV Scan can stay free

Affiliate Disclosure

EV Scan is intended to remain free for buyers. One way we may fund the service is through clearly identified automotive partner and affiliate links.

What an affiliate link means

If a link is marked as an ad, affiliate or partner link, EV Scan may receive a commission or referral payment if you click it, request a quote or purchase a service. This should not normally increase the price you pay.

How we label commercial links

Commercial intent should be clear before you engage with a link. Where required, individual affiliate cards or links will be labelled with wording such as Ad, Affiliate or Partner, rather than relying only on this disclosure page.

What commission does not change

Affiliate commission must not change:

  • a vehicle’s Deal Score;
  • Decision Confidence;
  • MOT interpretation;
  • battery or range estimates;
  • whether EV Scan says information is missing or unknown; or
  • the substantive recommendation about whether a vehicle looks worth investigating.

If a paid partner is shown as a useful next step, that commercial relationship will be kept separate from the vehicle-scoring logic.

Choosing a provider

A partner appearing on EV Scan is not a guarantee that it is the cheapest or only suitable provider. You should compare services and check the provider’s own terms, privacy notice, eligibility requirements and pricing before purchasing.

Current prototype

Some partner areas in the prototype may be placeholders and may not yet contain live affiliate links. EV Scan will not claim a commercial relationship exists until one has actually been agreed.

Privacy & device storage

Cookies & Local Storage

EV Scan keeps tracking deliberately minimal. This page explains the device storage used by the service and the strictly necessary session cookie used only when you choose to sign in.

Current position

No intentional behavioural advertising cookiesEV Scan does not currently intentionally set behavioural advertising cookies.
No intentional optional analytics cookiesOptional analytics cookies are not currently required to use EV Scan.
Account session cookieIf you log in, EV Scan sets a secure, HTTP-only session cookie so you can remain signed in. It is used for authentication, not advertising, and is treated as necessary for the optional account feature to work.
Local browser storageSome features may use localStorage on your device, for example to remember locally submitted feedback or interface state.
InfrastructureOur hosting/security provider may use strictly necessary technologies to deliver, protect and route the service.

Account session cookie

The account cookie contains a random session token rather than your password. It is marked Secure, HttpOnly and SameSite=Lax. Current sessions are time-limited and may be ended earlier when you log out or delete your account.

You can use EV Scan’s core scanning features without creating an account, so no account session is needed unless you choose to log in.

What is local storage?

Local storage is a browser technology that can save small amounts of information on your device. Similar privacy rules can apply to cookies and other technologies that store or access information on a device.

You can remove EV Scan local storage using your browser’s site-data or privacy controls. Doing so may remove locally saved interface state or prototype feedback. Account information stored on the server is managed separately through My EV Scan.

What happens when analytics or ads are introduced?

Before EV Scan activates non-essential cookies or similar tracking technologies that require consent, we will:

  • update this notice with the specific technologies, providers, purposes and durations;
  • show an appropriate consent control before non-essential tracking begins;
  • keep optional tracking switched off unless the required consent has been given; and
  • make it possible to change or withdraw choices.

Affiliate links

An affiliate link can contain a tracking identifier so a partner knows EV Scan referred a visitor. Clicking an affiliate link may cause the partner website to set its own cookies or similar technologies under its own privacy/cookie information. Affiliate links on EV Scan will be clearly identified where required.

Changes

This page will be reviewed before any analytics, ad network, conversion tracking or other non-essential tracking is enabled.

Privacy & data

Privacy Notice

EV Scan is designed to work with as little personal information as possible. This notice explains what information the current service uses, why it is used, and what changes when optional features such as partners, analytics or live marketplace integrations are enabled.

Prototype status: EV Scan is currently an early-stage UK project. A dedicated privacy contact email must be published here before a wider public launch. Until then, do not submit sensitive personal information through the service.

1. Who is responsible for the service?

EV Scan is the project responsible for the website and the processing described in this notice. The service is operated in the United Kingdom.

Website: ev-scan-mvp.alfieparkerfellows.workers.dev

Privacy contact: a dedicated contact address will be added before public launch.

2. Information we currently use

Vehicle informationRegistration numbers, listing URLs and vehicle details submitted for a scan.
Insurance estimator answersDriver and usage answers entered into the estimator. The current prototype calculates these in the browser and is not intended to store them on a server.
FeedbackStar ratings and optional comments may be stored locally on the device in the prototype. If server-side review storage is introduced, this notice will be updated before collection starts.
Technical logsOur hosting provider may process request information such as IP address, timestamps, browser/network information and security logs to deliver and protect the service.

3. Why we use information

We use information to provide the scan or feature requested, operate and secure the website, diagnose faults, prevent abuse, and improve the service.

Where personal data is processed to provide a feature you request, the relevant lawful basis will normally be that the processing is necessary to provide that service. Where limited technical data is used to secure and improve the service, we may rely on legitimate interests, balanced against users’ privacy rights. If we introduce optional analytics, advertising or other processing that requires consent, we will ask for that consent before it begins.

4. Vehicle and third-party data

Vehicle information may come from information you provide and authorised third-party sources such as DVSA or approved marketplace/data providers. A registration number is generally about a vehicle rather than a person, but it can become personal data in some circumstances when combined with other information. We therefore handle submitted registrations carefully and do not use them to identify vehicle owners.

5. Who information may be shared with

Information may be processed by infrastructure and service providers needed to run EV Scan, including our web hosting/CDN provider and authorised vehicle-data providers when a live lookup is requested. We do not sell user data to advertisers.

Affiliate partners receive information only when you choose to follow a partner link, subject to that partner’s own privacy notice. EV Scan does not send your insurance-estimator answers to an insurer unless a future feature clearly tells you this and you choose to proceed.

6. How long we keep information

The current prototype is intentionally low-retention. Browser-only data remains on your device until you clear it or the feature removes it. Hosting and security logs may be retained for limited periods by the infrastructure provider. If EV Scan introduces accounts, server-side reviews or stored scan history, specific retention periods will be published before those features are enabled.

7. International processing

Some infrastructure or third-party providers may process data outside the UK. Where this involves personal data, appropriate UK data-protection safeguards must be used. The exact providers and transfer arrangements will be documented as production integrations are finalised.

8. Your rights

Depending on the circumstances, UK data-protection law may give you rights to access, correct, erase or restrict use of your personal data, object to certain processing, and receive certain data in a portable format. Where processing relies on consent, you can withdraw that consent.

You also have the right to complain to the UK Information Commissioner’s Office if you believe your data has been handled unlawfully.

9. Automated scores and estimates

EV Scan may automatically generate Deal Scores, confidence indicators, range estimates, insurance estimates and other buying-assistance outputs. These are informational tools, not decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects on you. We label estimated, seller-supplied, verified and unknown information separately wherever practicable.

10. Changes to this notice

We will update this notice when the service changes, particularly before introducing accounts, persistent review storage, analytics, personalised advertising, new data providers or new uses of personal information.

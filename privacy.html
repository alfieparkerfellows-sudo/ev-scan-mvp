Privacy & data

Privacy Notice

EV Scan is designed to work without an account. If you choose to create a free account, we store only the information needed to remember your scans, shortlist, driving preferences and ownership dashboard.

Prototype status: EV Scan is currently an early-stage UK project. A dedicated privacy contact email must be published here before a wider public launch. Until then, do not submit sensitive personal information through the service.

1. Who is responsible for the service?

EV Scan is the project responsible for the website and the processing described in this notice. The service is operated in the United Kingdom.

Website: ev-scan-mvp.alfieparkerfellows.workers.dev

Privacy contact: a dedicated contact address will be added before public launch.

2. Information we use

Vehicle informationRegistration numbers, listing URLs, vehicle details and scan results submitted or saved through EV Scan.
Optional account informationIf you create an account, we store your email address, optional display name, account preferences, saved scans, shortlist choices, driving profile and vehicles/dates you choose to add to My Garage.
Password securityYour password is not stored in readable form. EV Scan stores a salted password hash used to verify future sign-ins.
Ownership remindersMOT, service, tax/vehicle-duty and battery-warranty dates are stored only when you add them to My Garage. Current reminders are shown in the app and can be exported to your own calendar.
Insurance estimator answersDriver and usage answers entered into the estimator are currently calculated in the browser and are not intended to be stored in your EV Scan account.
Feedback and usage dataEV Scan may collect limited product-usage events and feedback where enabled to understand reliability and improve the service. We aim to avoid collecting unnecessary identifying information in these events.
Technical logsOur hosting provider may process request information such as IP address, timestamps, browser/network information and security logs to deliver and protect the service.

3. Why we use information

We use information to provide the scan or feature requested, operate optional accounts, save the information you ask us to remember, provide My Garage and reminder features, secure the service, diagnose faults, prevent abuse and improve EV Scan.

Where personal data is needed to provide an account or feature you request, the relevant lawful basis will normally be performance of the service you have asked us to provide. Where limited technical or anti-abuse data is used to secure and improve the service, we may rely on legitimate interests, balanced against users’ privacy rights. If we introduce optional analytics, advertising or other processing that requires consent, we will ask for that consent before it begins.

4. Account security and sessions

EV Scan account sessions use a secure, HTTP-only session cookie so the browser can remain signed in without exposing the session token to normal page JavaScript. Sessions are time-limited. Authentication attempts may be temporarily recorded in a privacy-minimised form to limit repeated login abuse.

You are responsible for choosing a strong password and keeping access to your email/device secure. Do not reuse a password that protects an important account elsewhere.

5. Vehicle and third-party data

Vehicle information may come from information you provide and authorised third-party sources such as DVSA or approved marketplace/data providers. A registration number is generally about a vehicle rather than a person, but it can become personal data in some circumstances when combined with other information. We therefore handle submitted registrations carefully and do not use them to identify vehicle owners.

6. Who information may be shared with

Information may be processed by infrastructure and service providers needed to run EV Scan, including our web hosting/database provider and authorised vehicle-data providers when a live lookup is requested. We do not sell user data to advertisers.

Affiliate partners receive information only when you choose to follow a partner link, subject to that partner’s own privacy notice. EV Scan does not send your insurance-estimator answers to an insurer unless a future feature clearly tells you this and you choose to proceed.

7. How long we keep information

If you do not create an account, EV Scan continues to minimise persistent personal information. If you create an account, your account profile, saved scans and My Garage information are kept while the account exists so those features work when you return. Authentication sessions are time-limited and old anti-abuse attempt records are periodically removed.

You can download a copy of the account data EV Scan exposes to you and permanently delete your account from My EV Scan → Preferences. Account deletion removes the active account records used by the service. Infrastructure providers may retain limited security logs or backups for their normal operational periods.

8. International processing

Some infrastructure or third-party providers may process data outside the UK. Where this involves personal data, appropriate UK data-protection safeguards must be used. The exact providers and transfer arrangements will be documented as production integrations are finalised.

9. Your rights

Depending on the circumstances, UK data-protection law may give you rights to access, correct, erase or restrict use of your personal data, object to certain processing, and receive certain data in a portable format. Where processing relies on consent, you can withdraw that consent.

Logged-in users can use the built-in data export and account deletion controls. You also have the right to complain to the UK Information Commissioner’s Office if you believe your data has been handled unlawfully.

10. Automated scores and estimates

EV Scan may automatically generate Deal Scores, confidence indicators, range estimates, insurance estimates and other buying-assistance outputs. These are informational tools, not decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects on you. We label estimated, seller-supplied, verified and unknown information separately wherever practicable.

11. Changes to this notice

We will update this notice as the service changes, particularly before introducing new notification delivery, personalised advertising, material new data providers or new uses of personal information.
